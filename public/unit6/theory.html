3. Trigonometric Identities

Pythagorean Identity 1

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
→ sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ | cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ

Pythagorean Identity 2

1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
→ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1

Pythagorean Identity 3

1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
→ cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1

4. Complementary Angles

  • sin(90° − θ) = cos θ
  • cos(90° − θ) = sin θ
  • tan(90° − θ) = cot θ
  • cot(90° − θ) = tan θ
  • sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ
  • cosec(90° − θ) = sec θ

5. Heights and Distances

Angle of Elevation

The angle formed when you look upward from the horizontal to an object. Measured from the horizontal line up to the line of sight.

Angle of Depression

The angle formed when you look downward from the horizontal to an object. Measured from the horizontal line down to the line of sight.

Angle of elevation from A to B = Angle of depression from B to A (alternate interior angles with the horizontal as transversal).

Steps to Solve Heights & Distances Problems

  • Draw a clear diagram with right-angled triangles.
  • Mark the known distances and angles.
  • Identify which trig ratio connects the known and unknown sides.
  • Set up the equation and solve.
