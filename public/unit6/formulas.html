Pythagorean Identities

Identity 1
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
→ sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ | cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ
Identity 2
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
→ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 | tan²θ = sec²θ − 1
Identity 3
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
→ cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1 | cot²θ = cosec²θ − 1

Reciprocal Relations

Reciprocals
sin θ · cosec θ = 1 cos θ · sec θ = 1 tan θ · cot θ = 1
Each trig ratio and its reciprocal multiply to 1.
tan and cot in terms of sin/cos
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ cot θ = cos θ / sin θ

Complementary Angles

Complementary Angle Identities
sin(90°−θ) = cosθ cos(90°−θ) = sinθ tan(90°−θ) = cotθ cot(90°−θ) = tanθ sec(90°−θ) = cosecθ cosec(90°−θ) = secθ

Heights & Distances

When angle of elevation = θ, height = h, distance = d
tan θ = h / d → h = d · tan θ sin θ = h / L → L = h / sin θ (L = line of sight)
Angle of elevation from A to B = Angle of depression from B to A
