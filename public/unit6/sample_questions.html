Ratios & Standard Values

1If sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ and tan θ.2 M
sin θ = 3/5 → opposite = 3, hypotenuse = 5 adjacent = √(5²−3²) = √16 = 4 cos θ = 4/5 tan θ = 3/4
2Evaluate: sin²30° + cos²60° + tan²45°2 M
sin30° = 1/2 → sin²30° = 1/4 cos60° = 1/2 → cos²60° = 1/4 tan45° = 1 → tan²45° = 1 Total = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1 = 3/2
3Simplify: sin 65° + cos 25°1 M
cos 25° = sin(90°−25°) = sin 65° So: sin 65° + sin 65° = 2 sin 65°

Trigonometric Identities

4If sec θ + tan θ = 3, find sec θ − tan θ.2 M
Using: sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 (sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ − tan θ) = 1 3 × (sec θ − tan θ) = 1 sec θ − tan θ = 1/3
5Prove: (sin θ + cosec θ)² + (cos θ + sec θ)² = 7 + tan²θ + cot²θ5 M
LHS = sin²θ + 2sinθ·cosecθ + cosec²θ + cos²θ + 2cosθ·secθ + sec²θ = sin²θ + cos²θ + 2(1) + cosec²θ + 2(1) + sec²θ = 1 + 2 + cosec²θ + 2 + sec²θ = 5 + (1+cot²θ) + (1+tan²θ) = 5 + 1 + cot²θ + 1 + tan²θ = 7 + tan²θ + cot²θ = RHS ✓
6Prove: tan θ/(1−cot θ) + cot θ/(1−tan θ) = 1 + sec θ · cosec θ5 M
Let t = tan θ, so cot θ = 1/t. LHS = t/(1−1/t) + (1/t)/(1−t) = t/((t−1)/t) + (1/t)/(1−t) = t²/(t−1) − 1/(t(t−1)) = (t³−1) / (t(t−1)) = (t−1)(t²+t+1) / (t(t−1)) = (t²+t+1)/t = t + 1 + 1/t = tan θ + 1 + cot θ = 1 + sin θ/cos θ + cos θ/sin θ = 1 + (sin²θ + cos²θ)/(sin θ cos θ) = 1 + 1/(sin θ cos θ) = 1 + sec θ · cosec θ = RHS ✓

Heights & Distances

7A tower casts a 40 m shadow when the angle of elevation of the sun is 30°. Find the height of the tower.3 M
tan 30° = height / distance 1/√3 = h/40 h = 40/√3 = 40√3/3 ≈ 23.1 m
8From the top of a cliff 60 m high, the angle of depression of a boat is 45°. Find the distance of the boat from the base of the cliff.3 M
Angle of depression = 45° ∴ Angle of elevation from boat to cliff top = 45° tan 45° = 60/d → 1 = 60/d d = 60 m
9From a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower is 30°. After walking 20 m toward the tower, it becomes 60°. Find the height.5 M
Let h = height, d = distance after walking 20 m closer. tan 30° = h/(d+20) → d+20 = h√3 ... (1) tan 60° = h/d → d = h/√3 ... (2) Substituting (2) into (1): h/√3 + 20 = h√3 h√3 − h/√3 = 20 h(3−1)/√3 = 20 → 2h/√3 = 20 h = 10√3 ≈ 17.3 m
10Two buildings are 60 m apart. The angle of elevation of the top of the taller from the base of the shorter is 60°, and vice versa is 30°. Find both heights.5 M
Let heights be H (taller) and h (shorter), distance = 60m. tan 60° = H/60 → H = 60√3 m tan 30° = h/60 → h = 60/√3 = 20√3 m Taller building = 60√3 ≈ 103.9 m Shorter building = 20√3 ≈ 34.6 m
