Cylinder, Cone & Sphere

1Find the CSA and TSA of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm.2 M
CSA = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm² TSA = 2πr(r+h) = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × (7+10) = 2 × 22 × 17 = 748 cm²
2Find the volume of a cone with radius 6 cm and height 7 cm.2 M
V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × 22/7 × 36 × 7 = (1/3) × 22 × 36 = 264 cm³
3Find the surface area and volume of a sphere of radius 7 cm.2 M
SA = 4πr² = 4 × 22/7 × 49 = 616 cm² V = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3) × 22/7 × 343 = 4 × 22 × 49/3 ≈ 1437.3 cm³
4Find the TSA of a hemisphere of radius 7 cm.2 M
TSA = 3πr² = 3 × 22/7 × 49 = 3 × 22 × 7 = 462 cm²
5A cone has radius 5 cm and height 12 cm. Find its slant height and CSA.3 M
l = √(r²+h²) = √(25+144) = √169 = 13 cm CSA = πrl = 22/7 × 5 × 13 = 1430/7 ≈ 204.3 cm²

Frustum & Combined Solids

6Find the volume of a frustum with R = 10 cm, r = 5 cm, h = 9 cm.3 M
V = (πh/3)(R² + r² + Rr) = (22/7 × 9/3)(100 + 25 + 50) = (22/7 × 3)(175) = 22 × 75 = 1650 cm³
7A toy is in the shape of a hemisphere surmounted by a cone, both of radius 3.5 cm. Height of cone = 4 cm. Find the total volume.4 M
r = 3.5 cm Volume of hemisphere = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × 22/7 × 42.875 = 89.83 cm³ Volume of cone = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × 22/7 × 12.25 × 4 = 51.33 cm³ Total = 89.83 + 51.33 = 141.17 cm³ ≈ 141.2 cm³

Conversion of Solids

8A solid sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast into a cone of radius 3 cm. Find the height of the cone.3 M
Volume of sphere = Volume of cone (4/3)πr³ = (1/3)πr²h (4/3)π(27) = (1/3)π(9)h 36π = 3πh h = 12 cm
9How many small spheres of radius 1 cm can be made from a large sphere of radius 4 cm?2 M
n = (R/r)³ = (4/1)³ = 64 spheres
10A metallic cube of side 6 cm is melted and cast into a cylinder of radius 3 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.3 M
Volume of cube = Volume of cylinder 6³ = πr²h 216 = 22/7 × 9 × h h = 216 × 7 / (22 × 9) = 1512/198 ≈ 7.64 cm
