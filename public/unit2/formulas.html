Euclid's Division & HCF/LCM

Euclid's Division Lemma
a = bq + r, where 0 ≤ r < b
a = dividend, b = divisor, q = quotient, r = remainder. The pair (q, r) is unique for every pair (a, b).
HCF × LCM = Product of Two Numbers
HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b
Use: If HCF and the two numbers are known, find LCM = (a × b) / HCF.
Valid only for exactly two positive integers.

Arithmetic Progression (AP)

nth Term of an AP
aₙ = a + (n − 1)d
a = first term, d = common difference, n = term number.
Common difference: d = a₂ − a₁
Sum of n Terms of AP (using first term & d)
Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d]
Use when the first term a and common difference d are known.
Sum of n Terms of AP (using first & last term)
Sₙ = n/2 × (a + l)
l = last term (nth term). Use when both the first and last term are known.

Geometric Progression (GP)

nth Term of a GP
aₙ = a · rⁿ⁻¹
a = first term, r = common ratio (r ≠ 0), n = term number.
Common ratio: r = a₂ / a₁
Sum of n Terms of GP (r ≠ 1)
Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1) / (r − 1)
Use when r ≠ 1. If r > 1, use Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1). If r < 1, use Sₙ = a(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r).
Sum of n Terms of GP (r = 1)
Sₙ = n · a
When r = 1, all terms are equal to a, so the sum is simply na.

Special Sum Formulas

Sum of First n Natural Numbers
1 + 2 + 3 + … + n = n(n + 1) / 2
Example: Sum of first 100 natural numbers = 100 × 101 / 2 = 5050
Sum of Squares of First n Natural Numbers
1² + 2² + … + n² = n(n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6
Example: Σn² for n=5: 5 × 6 × 11 / 6 = 55
Sum of Cubes of First n Natural Numbers
1³ + 2³ + … + n³ = [n(n + 1) / 2]²
Key identity Σn³ = (Σn)². The sum of cubes equals the square of the sum of natural numbers.

Modular Arithmetic

Congruence Modulo m
a ≡ b (mod m) ⟺ m | (a − b)
a is congruent to b modulo m if m divides (a − b).
Example: 17 ≡ 2 (mod 5) because 5 | (17 − 2) = 15
Remainder Formula
a mod m = r (the remainder when a is divided by m)
The modulo operation gives the remainder. If a = mq + r, then a mod m = r where 0 ≤ r < m.
