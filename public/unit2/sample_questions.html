Euclid's Algorithm & HCF/LCM

1Find HCF(48, 18) using Euclid's Division Algorithm.2 M
Apply Euclid's Division Lemma repeatedly:
Step 1: 48 = 18 × 2 + 12
Step 2: 18 = 12 × 1 + 6
Step 3: 12 = 6 × 2 + 0
Remainder = 0, so HCF(48, 18) = 6
2The HCF of two numbers is 6 and their product is 216. Find the LCM.2 M
Using the formula: LCM × HCF = Product of two numbers
LCM × 6 = 216
LCM = 216 ÷ 6 = 36

Arithmetic Progression

3Find the 10th term of the AP: 2, 5, 8, 11, …2 M
First term a = 2, Common difference d = 5 − 2 = 3
nth term formula: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d
a₁₀ = 2 + (10 − 1) × 3 = 2 + 27 = 29
4Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the AP: 3, 7, 11, …3 M
a = 3, d = 7 − 3 = 4, n = 20
Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d]
S₂₀ = 20/2 × [2(3) + (20 − 1)(4)]
S₂₀ = 10 × [6 + 76]
S₂₀ = 10 × 82 = 820

Geometric Progression

5Find the 5th term of the GP: 2, 6, 18, …2 M
a = 2, r = 6 ÷ 2 = 3
nth term formula: aₙ = a · rⁿ⁻¹
a₅ = 2 × 3⁴ = 2 × 81 = 162
6Find the sum of the first 6 terms of the GP: 1, 2, 4, 8, …3 M
a = 1, r = 2, n = 6
Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1) / (r − 1)
S₆ = 1 × (2⁶ − 1) / (2 − 1)
S₆ = (64 − 1) / 1 = 63

Special Sum Formulas

7Find the sum of the first 15 natural numbers.2 M
Using the formula: Σn = n(n + 1) / 2
Sum = 15 × 16 / 2 = 240 / 2 = 120
8Find the sum of the squares of the first 5 natural numbers.2 M
Using the formula: Σn² = n(n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6
Sum = 5 × 6 × 11 / 6 = 330 / 6 = 55
Verify: 1² + 2² + 3² + 4² + 5² = 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + 25 = 55 ✓

Advanced Problems

9The 7th term of an AP is 40 and the 13th term is 70. Find the AP (first term and common difference).4 M
Let first term = a, common difference = d
7th term: a + 6d = 40 … (1)
13th term: a + 12d = 70 … (2)
Subtract (1) from (2): 6d = 30 → d = 5
Substitute in (1): a + 30 = 40 → a = 10
The AP is: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, …
10In a GP, the 3rd term is 24 and the 6th term is 192. Find the first term and common ratio.4 M
Let first term = a, common ratio = r
3rd term: ar² = 24 … (1)
6th term: ar⁵ = 192 … (2)
Divide (2) by (1): r³ = 192/24 = 8 → r = 2
Substitute in (1): a × 4 = 24 → a = 6
The GP is: 6, 12, 24, 48, 96, 192, …
