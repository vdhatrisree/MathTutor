Basic Proportionality Theorem

1In △ABC, DE ∥ BC. If AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm, and AE = 5 cm, find EC.2 M
By the Basic Proportionality Theorem (DE ∥ BC):
AD/DB = AE/EC
4/6 = 5/EC
EC = (5 × 6) / 4 = 30/4 = 7.5 cm
2In △PQR, a line DE is drawn such that PD = 3, DQ = 4.5, PE = 4, ER = 6. Is DE ∥ QR? Justify.3 M
Check if PD/DQ = PE/ER:
PD/DQ = 3/4.5 = 2/3
PE/ER = 4/6 = 2/3
Since PD/DQ = PE/ER = 2/3, by the converse of BPT, DE ∥ QR. ✓

Similar Triangles

3In △ABC and △DEF, ∠A = ∠D = 50° and ∠B = ∠E = 70°. Are the triangles similar? If △ABC ~ △DEF and AB = 6 cm, DE = 4 cm, find BC if EF = 3 cm.3 M
∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E, so by AA criterion, △ABC ~ △DEF.
Since the triangles are similar: AB/DE = BC/EF
6/4 = BC/3
BC = (6 × 3) / 4 = 18/4 = 4.5 cm
4△ABC ~ △DEF. The areas of △ABC and △DEF are 64 cm² and 25 cm² respectively. If BC = 8 cm, find EF.3 M
For similar triangles: Area(△ABC)/Area(△DEF) = (BC/EF)²
64/25 = (8/EF)²
(8/EF)² = 64/25
8/EF = 8/5
EF = (8 × 5)/8 = 5 cm

Pythagoras Theorem

5In a right-angled triangle, the two legs are 5 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.2 M
By Pythagoras Theorem: c² = a² + b²
c² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169
c = √169 = 13 cm
6Check if a triangle with sides 7 cm, 24 cm, and 25 cm is a right-angled triangle.2 M
Check the converse of Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²?
7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625
25² = 625
Since 7² + 24² = 25², by the converse of Pythagoras Theorem, the triangle is right-angled (right angle opposite the 25 cm side).

Tangents to a Circle

7From a point P outside a circle, the distance to the centre O is 13 cm. If the radius is 5 cm, find the length of the tangent from P to the circle.2 M
Using the tangent length formula: t = √(d² − r²)
t = √(13² − 5²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 cm
The tangent length is 12 cm.
8Two tangents PA and PB are drawn from an external point P to a circle with centre O. If PA = 10 cm, find PB. Also find ∠OAP if OP = 26 cm.3 M
Since tangents from an external point are equal: PB = PA = 10 cm

For ∠OAP: OA ⊥ PA (radius ⊥ tangent), so △OAP is right-angled at A.
OA² = OP² − PA² = 26² − 10² = 676 − 100 = 576
OA = 24 cm (radius)
∠OAP = 90° (tangent is perpendicular to radius at point of contact)
9AB is a diameter of a circle with centre O. C is a point on the circle. If ∠BAC = 35°, find ∠ABC and ∠ACB.4 M
Since AB is a diameter, by the Angle in a Semicircle theorem:
∠ACB = 90° (angle in semicircle)

In △ABC: ∠BAC + ∠ABC + ∠ACB = 180°
35° + ∠ABC + 90° = 180°
∠ABC = 180° − 125° = 55°
10In △ABC, right-angled at B, a circle is inscribed with centre O and radius r. If AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, AC = 5 cm, find the radius of the inscribed circle.5 M
For a right-angled triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c:
Inradius r = (a + b − c) / 2
r = (AB + BC − AC) / 2
r = (3 + 4 − 5) / 2
r = 2/2 = 1 cm

Alternatively, using Area method:
Area of △ABC = (1/2) × 3 × 4 = 6 cm²
Semi-perimeter s = (3 + 4 + 5)/2 = 6
Area = r × s → 6 = r × 6 → r = 1 cm ✓
