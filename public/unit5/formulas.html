Distance & Points

Distance Formula
d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
Distance between points A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂).
Distance from Origin
d = √(x² + y²)
Distance of point P(x, y) from origin O(0, 0).
Midpoint Formula
M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
Midpoint of segment joining (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂).
Centroid of Triangle
G = ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3)
Centroid of triangle with vertices (x₁,y₁), (x₂,y₂), (x₃,y₃).

Section Formula

Internal Division (ratio m : n)
P = ( (mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n) , (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n) )
Point P divides segment AB internally in ratio m : n, where A = (x₁,y₁) and B = (x₂,y₂).
External Division (ratio m : n)
P = ( (mx₂ − nx₁)/(m−n) , (my₂ − ny₁)/(m−n) )
Point P divides segment AB externally in ratio m : n (P lies outside AB). m ≠ n.

Area

Area of Triangle
Area = ½ |x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂)|
Vertices: A(x₁,y₁), B(x₂,y₂), C(x₃,y₃). Area = 0 means points are collinear.
Condition for Collinearity
x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂) = 0
Three points are collinear if this expression equals zero.

Slope

Slope between Two Points
m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
Also written as: m = tan θ, where θ is the inclination angle.
Slope from General Form ax+by+c=0
m = −a/b
Rearrange to y = (−a/b)x + (−c/b) to identify slope and intercept.
Parallel Lines Condition
m₁ = m₂
Lines are parallel if they have equal slopes.
Perpendicular Lines Condition
m₁ × m₂ = −1
Lines are perpendicular if product of slopes = −1.

Equations of a Line

Slope-Intercept Form
y = mx + c
m = slope, c = y-intercept.
Point-Slope Form
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
Line with slope m passing through point (x₁, y₁).
Two-Point Form
(y − y₁)/(y₂ − y₁) = (x − x₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
Line passing through (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂).
Intercept Form
x/a + y/b = 1
a = x-intercept, b = y-intercept.
General Form
ax + by + c = 0
Every straight line can be written in this form.
Perpendicular Distance from Point to Line
d = |ax₁ + by₁ + c| / √(a² + b²)
Distance from point (x₁,y₁) to line ax + by + c = 0.
Quick Reference: For any line y = mx + c  →  slope = m, y-intercept = c, x-intercept = −c/m.
Parallel to y = mx + c: any line y = mx + k (k ≠ c).  |  Perpendicular to y = mx + c: slope = −1/m.
